Informed Consent

Study Purpose

We're investigating how explanations affect user interaction with movie recommender systems. This study compares two versions of our cold-start recommender: one that provides explanations about how your ratings influence future recommendations, and one that doesn't.

What You'll Do

You'll start by rating a diverse set of movies, then receive personalized recommendations. The entire process takes approximately 10-15 minutes.

Data Collection & Processing

We will collect the following data during your participation:

  • Movie ratings you provide during the study
  • Interaction timestamps and response times
  • Study condition assignment (guided vs. standard)
  • Anonymous session identifier (no personal information)

Data Use & Storage

Your data will be used solely for academic research purposes. All data is:

  • Anonymous: No personally identifiable information is collected
  • Secure: Stored on encrypted servers with restricted access
  • Retained: For up to 5 years for research analysis
  • Shared: Only in aggregated, anonymized form for research publications

Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Withdraw from the study at any time without consequence
  • Request information about your data processing
  • Contact us with data protection concerns
