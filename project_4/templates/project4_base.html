About This Study

We're investigating how explanations affect user interaction with movie recommender systems. This study compares two versions of our cold-start recommender: one that provides explanations about how your ratings influence future recommendations, and one that doesn't.

You'll start by rating a diverse set of movies, then receive personalized recommendations. The entire process takes approximately 10-15 minutes.

Study Information: Duration: 10-15 minutes | Requirements: Basic familiarity with movies | Data: Anonymous and GDPR compliant

Choose Your Study Condition

Please select one of the two study conditions below. Both conditions are equally important for our research.

🔍 Guided Experience
See detailed explanations about how your ratings will influence future movie recommendations. Understand the impact of your choices on the recommendation algorithm.
Start Guided Study
⚡ Standard Experience
Rate movies and receive recommendations without additional explanations. Experience the recommender system in its streamlined form.
Start Standard Study

Research Context

This study is part of ongoing research into explainable AI and human-computer interaction in recommender systems. Your participation helps us understand how transparency affects user experience and system performance.
